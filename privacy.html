Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

This website is run by Sovereign Labs (“we”, “us”) and presents Verity, our intelligence software for law firms. This policy explains what personal data we handle when you visit this website or use its request-access form. It does not cover the Verity software itself, which is provided to firms under separate agreements.

1. What we collect

Information you give us. If you use the request-access form, we receive your name, work email address and firm name, plus anything else you choose to tell us.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, the servers that deliver this site may log standard technical details such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request. This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your request and, where appropriate, arrange access to Verity.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries from businesses and on taking steps you ask for before entering into an agreement. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who else receives it

  • Google Fonts. Your browser loads this site’s typefaces from Google’s servers, which therefore receive your IP address.
  • jsDelivr. Your browser loads an open-source graphics library (three.js) from the jsDelivr content delivery network, which therefore receives your IP address.
  • Hosting and form-handling providers. The services that host this site and process the request-access form handle the data described above on our behalf.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it. Some of these providers may process data outside your country.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your request and any resulting relationship, and then delete or anonymise them.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, and to complain to your data protection regulator. To make a privacy request, contact us using the request-access form and say that it is a privacy request.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed at children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top.

9. Contact

Sovereign Labs, via the request-access form on this website. See also our Terms of Use.
