Core Intelligence

Three ideas. One platform.

Verity is built on three interlocking principles. Each one makes the others more powerful.

01 Relationships

Verity connects the dots.

Verity maps every connection surrounding the firm — going far beyond what has been recorded. It looks across the full network of people and entities your firm touches.

Clients → People → Companies → Investors → +more

A relationship that appears insignificant on its own may become important when placed inside the larger network. Verity’s job is to connect the dots.

Every claim is traceable.
